How to play the game:

Controls:

W	- Move up,
A	- Move left
D	- Move right
S	- move down

W+D - move diagonally right up
W+A - Move diagonally left up
S+A - Move diagonally down left
S+D - Move diagonally down right

Weapons are auto firing
Aim mouse in direction to fire

Health bar takes damage when enemy touches you.
You only have 1 life.

The EXP bar is the blue bar at the top of the screen.
When EXP bar fills up, you will level up and a skill menu will pop up.

Skill menu:

You can unlock new weapons or upgrade existing weapons here.
Only one selection at a time.
Fill the EXP bar again to make another selection.
